Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use NumberMerge 2048, why, and the choices you have. It covers:

  • the NumberMerge 2048 Android app (Google Play package com.hadify.NumberMerge2048), and
  • this website, 2048.hadify.app.

NumberMerge 2048 is made by Hadify Studios ("we", "us"), an independent developer. For the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR, Hadify Studios is the controller of the personal data described here. Google is a separate, independent controller for the advertising data it collects through its ads SDK (see section 3). You can contact us at hadivahidi20@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud save. Your game progress is stored on your device and is never sent to us.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob: banner ads, and rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one for coins. To show and measure ads, Google collects some device and ad data.
  • In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are personalised. In US states with privacy laws, it lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money. We don't use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools in the app or on the website.

1. What information is handled and why

a) Game data on your device. Your coins, best score, saved boards, power-up charges, daily-challenge progress, board size, difficulty and sound setting are stored in the app's private storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences). We never receive this data. If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include it in your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. Google runs that backup under its own terms, and we can't access it. Advertising and consent data are not included in the app's backup.

b) Sharing a score. If you tap Share, the app creates an image of your board in its temporary cache and passes it, with a short message, to the app you pick (for example a messaging app). Nothing is sent to us.

c) Advertising — Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (and app set ID),
  • your IP address, which is used to estimate your approximate location (for example your country or city, not your precise location),
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version, and
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped, plus diagnostic information about the ads SDK.

d) Your privacy choices. Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) saves your consent or opt-out choice on your device, for example as an industry-standard consent string, so that the app and Google can respect it.

e) This website. 2048.hadify.app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address, the pages you request, your browser type and the time of each request, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser also connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. The website has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

What we don't collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera. There are no real-money purchases, so we never handle payment details. Coins are earned in the game and have no monetary value. The app asks for no runtime permissions and sends no notifications.

2. Legal bases (UK GDPR and EU GDPR)

  • Consent — for storing and reading information on your device for advertising and for personalised ads, where the law requires consent (including the UK PECR and the EU ePrivacy rules). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Legitimate interests — to show non-personalised or limited ads so the game can stay free, to measure ads and prevent ad fraud, and to keep the website secure. You can object to this (see section 7).

3. Advertising and your choices

  • Personalised ads may use your advertising ID and your activity across apps and websites to choose ads that match your interests.
  • Non-personalised ads are not based on your past behaviour. They are chosen from context, such as the app you are using and your approximate location. Google still uses some data for them, for example to limit how often you see an ad, to measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: when you first open the app (version 1.0.5 and later), Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related purposes. If you don't agree, you get non-personalised or limited ads.
  • US states with privacy laws: where it applies, the same Google tool shows a US state privacy message that lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Change your choice: open Settings → Privacy options in the app. This entry appears wherever a privacy law requires it. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You only see one if you tap "Watch ad" to earn coins.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Who receives information

  • Google — AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent), Google Play (app distribution) and Google Fonts (website fonts).
  • Cloudflare — website hosting.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws may treat letting AdMob use your information for personalised ads as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. See section 8 for how to opt out.

5. International transfers

Google and Cloudflare may process data in the United States and other countries. For data from the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, they rely on legal safeguards: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and/or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission and the UK.

6. How long data is kept

  • Game data on your device: until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Shared board images are temporary files in the app's cache.
  • Advertising data: Google keeps it for limited periods under its own policies. See how Google retains data.
  • Website logs: Cloudflare keeps security and server logs for limited periods.

We don't keep personal data about you on servers of our own.

7. Your rights (UK, EEA and Switzerland)

You have the right to access your personal data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how it is used, and receive it in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Because your game data stays on your device and we can't link advertising data to you, we usually hold no data that identifies you, but we will always help: email hadivahidi20@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); in the EEA, the authority in your country; in Switzerland, the FDPIC.

8. United States privacy

Children (COPPA). NumberMerge 2048 is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

California and other state privacy rights. Depending on where you live, US state privacy laws give you rights over your personal information. These include the CCPA as amended by the CPRA in California, and similar laws in states such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon. You have the right to:

  • know and access the personal information collected about you,
  • delete it,
  • correct it, and
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information, and of targeted advertising.

We will not treat you differently for using these rights. We don't collect "sensitive" personal information.

Categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app:

  • identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID, IP address),
  • internet and device activity (ad interactions, device and app information), and
  • approximate location (estimated from your IP address).

This information comes from your device. It is used to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to keep the service secure, and it is disclosed to Google, our advertising partner. Your game data is stored only on your device and is not collected by us.

How to opt out of personalised ads:

  • open Settings → Privacy options in the app (Google's consent tool shows the GDPR or US state message that applies to you), or
  • use your device's ad settings to delete or reset your advertising ID.

The website shows no ads and uses no tracking, so there is nothing to opt out of there.

How to make a request: email hadivahidi20@gmail.com. We may ask for information to verify your request. An authorised agent can make a request for you if they have your written permission. If we turn down your request, you can appeal by replying with the subject "Privacy appeal".

9. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Ad requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app or on this website.
