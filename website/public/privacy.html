Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NumberMerge 2048 mobile game ("the app", "we", "us"), published by Hadify Studios, handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • On-device game data — your scores, best tile, coin balance, power-up charges, daily-challenge progress, board size and difficulty settings are stored locally on your device (Android SharedPreferences). This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to us.
  • Advertising identifier — our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect your device's advertising ID and related data to show and measure ads. See section 3.

The app has no account, no login and no cloud sync. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any sensitive personal data.

2. How we use information

  • To save and restore your game progress and settings on your device.
  • To display advertising that helps keep the game free.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID and ad-interaction data to provide, personalize and measure ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data.

4. Third-party services

We rely on the following third-party service, governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising, provided by Google.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Because the app stores progress only on your device and uses no accounts, the only data leaving your device is the advertising data handled by Google AdMob as described above.

6. Data retention & deletion

All game data is stored locally. It is removed when you uninstall the app, clear the app's storage in your device settings, or use any in-app reset option. To reset your advertising ID, use your device's Google Ads settings.

7. Children's privacy

NumberMerge 2048 is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will address it.

8. Security

Game data is kept in your device's private app storage. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hadivahidi20@gmail.com.
